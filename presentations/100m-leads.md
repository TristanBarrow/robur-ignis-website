## Words are important.

They shape the way we think.

---

## What IS a lead?

--

Someone you can contact.

---

## What is an engaged lead?

--

Someone who is interested in my offer.

---

## Lead Magnets

A lead magnet attracts someone who is interested in my thing.

---

## Offer vs Magnet

**Offer:** Big problem solved traded for money.

**Magnet:** Little related problem solved for free to attract to the big problem.

(Nothing is free, they are still paying with their time)
